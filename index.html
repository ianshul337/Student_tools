About StudentTools

StudentTools is a simple collection of free browser-based utilities made for students. The goal is to make common calculations and small tasks quick and easy on mobile or desktop.

Available tools include percentage, CGPA, age, word counting, QR generation and image compression.

Privacy Policy

Most StudentTools utilities run directly in your browser. Calculator and text-tool inputs are processed locally by the page.

The QR Code Generator uses an external QR service to create the image, so the text or URL you enter may be sent to that service. Please do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

If analytics, advertising, accounts or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them.
